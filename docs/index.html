Product & technology

The essentials, without the noise.

Just the features you need to get organized, backed by an offline-first architecture: everything is stored on the device and works without a connection.

Truly offline

Lists, photos, audio and alarms live on your device. The app opens and works without a network.

Lists and checklists

Reorder with a long press, check off items and track each list's progress.

Photos, audio and QR

A gallery per reminder, voice notes and camera code scanning.

Alarms, themes and language

Local notifications, light/dark themes and copy in English and Spanish.

Under the hood

  • State Redux 5 + redux-persist with version migration and a single key.
  • Media Files copied to documentDirectory, with migration from cache.
  • Alarms Real UTC ISO and expo-notifications, without breaking on daylight saving time.
  • UI Reanimated 4, gestures and reorderable lists.
  • Distribution EAS Build + GitHub Releases. No Play Store, no account.

Design

Soft neumorphism, two themes.

A tactile interface that feels like a physical object. The relief isn't decoration: it separates, groups and gives feedback without shouting.

  • Theme transition with crossfade and Reanimated 4.
  • Glass header, zoomable gallery and custom modals.
  • Accessibility: roles, labels and states on every control.

Privacy by design

No servers, no analytics, no ads and no accounts. Your lists, photos and audio never leave the phone. You can delete everything by uninstalling the app.
